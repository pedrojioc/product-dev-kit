# Hexa Academy

## Vision
An online catalog where instructors publish courses and students enroll in them.

## Personas
- **Instructor**: publishes and retires courses.
- **Student**: finds a course and enrolls in it.

## Glossary (ubiquitous language)
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Course | A published unit of teaching, uniquely named while active |
| Enrollment | A student's active participation in one course |

## Modules
| Module | Summary | File |
|---|---|---|
| course | Publishing and retiring courses | modules/course.md |
| enrollment | Students joining and leaving a course | modules/enrollment.md |

## Global rules
- Every business date is recorded in UTC.

## Out of scope
- Payments.
